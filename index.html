Rules: exact batch number match. Carton Sent (column I) must be ticked or have a quantity; the word “Pend” does not count. Foil Qty Issued must be filled. Foil Qty Returned must be blank on every row for that batch; a typed 0 counts as filled. Tablets are checked on the TABLET and Beta Lectum tabs, capsules on the CAPSULE tabs, of both PM sheets.

ALU-ALU machine plan

Allots the matched ALU-ALU batches to ALU 1 to ALU 7 by change part, so each machine changes over as little as possible. Blister and strip are not planned yet.

Rules: exact batch number match. Carton Sent (column I) must be ticked or have a quantity; the word “Pend” does not count. Foil Qty Issued must be filled. Foil Qty Returned must be blank on every row for that batch; a typed 0 counts as filled. Tablets are checked on the TABLET and Beta Lectum tabs, capsules on the CAPSULE tabs, of the PM sheets named in the bookmark. Machine plan: ALU-ALU only, by foil size and tablet or capsule size; 185MM, 90MM and 210MM in FMS are treated as the 190MM, 92MM and 214MM change parts; load is counted in tablets or capsules.

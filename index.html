Machine plan

Allots the matched batches to the ALU-ALU, Blister and Strip machines by change part, so each machine changes over as little as possible, and gives every batch a start and end time.

Rules: exact batch number match. Carton Sent (column I) must be ticked or have a quantity; the word “Pend” does not count. Foil Qty Issued must be filled. Foil Qty Returned must be blank on every row for that batch; a typed 0 counts as filled. Tablets are checked on the TABLET and Beta Lectum tabs, capsules on the CAPSULE tabs, of the PM sheets named in the bookmark. Machine plan: change part is chosen by foil size and tablet or capsule size; LD foil means strip; 185MM, 90MM and 210MM in FMS are treated as the 190MM, 92MM and 214MM change parts; machines are balanced by running hours; each batch gets a start and end from its quantity and the time per lakh of its change part; batches matched on the Beta Lectum tab are left out of the machine plan.
